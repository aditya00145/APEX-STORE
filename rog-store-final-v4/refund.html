Return, Replacement & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy governs returns, replacements, and refunds for hardware purchased through APEX STORE (Apex Store India Pvt. Ltd., Dadar, Mumbai).

7-Day Replacement Guarantee: If your product arrives dead on arrival (DOA), physically damaged, or missing parts, we provide a free doorstep replacement or 100% refund within 7 days of delivery.

1. 7-Day Return & Replacement Window

All brand-new gaming hardware purchased from APEX STORE is backed by our 7-calendar-day return and replacement guarantee from the date of confirmed delivery. You are eligible for a replacement or full refund if:

  • The item arrives damaged, dented, or broken in transit.
  • The item is Dead on Arrival (DOA) or has a hardware manufacturing defect.
  • The delivered product differs in model, specifications, or color from what was ordered.
  • Key original accessories, cables, or power adapters are missing from the sealed box.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a hassle-free return or replacement, please ensure the following:

  • Original Packaging: The product must be returned with its original brand box, outer carton, user manuals, and warranty cards.
  • Serial Numbers & IMEI: The serial number, IMEI, and barcode on the device must match the original tax invoice issued by Apex Store India Pvt. Ltd.
  • No Tampering: The device must not have signs of physical misuse, liquid spills, unauthorized disassembly, or custom firmware flashing.
  • Unboxing Video: For transit damage or missing parts claims, sharing a continuous unboxing video recorded upon delivery helps expedite immediate resolution.

3. Step-by-Step Return Process

  1. Initiate Request: Message us on WhatsApp at +91 96656 87711 or email support@apexstore.in within 7 days with your Order ID (e.g. APEX-IND-######) and brief photos/video of the issue.
  2. Reverse Pickup: Our team will schedule a free reverse pickup via BlueDart or Delhivery from your address within 24 to 48 hours.
  3. Inspection & Verification: The item is inspected at our Dadar, Mumbai facility within 24 hours of arrival to verify the reported issue.
  4. Resolution: A brand-new replacement unit is dispatched immediately, or a full refund is initiated as per your preference.

4. Refund Mode & Timelines

Once your return is verified and approved at our Dadar fulfillment center, refunds are processed without any restocking fees:

  • UPI / QR Code Payments: Processed immediately and credited back to your linked bank account within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: We will request your preferred UPI ID or Bank NEFT account details. The refund is credited within 2 to 3 business days.
  • Debit / Credit Card Payments: Credited back to the issuing bank card within 4 to 7 business days depending on your card issuer.

5. Manufacturer Brand Warranty (Beyond 7 Days)

Beyond the initial 7-day replacement window, all products sold by APEX STORE carry complete 1-Year to 3-Year Official Brand Warranties across India. You can claim service at any authorized brand service center nationwide (including ASUS ROG, Lenovo, LG, Samsung, Sony, Apple, Razer, NVIDIA, and AMD) using your Apex Store tax invoice.
