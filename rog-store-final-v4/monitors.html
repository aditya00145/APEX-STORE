Buying guide

Refresh rate (Hz) controls how smooth motion looks, and your graphics card needs to keep up with it. IPS panels give accurate colour and wide viewing angles. OLED panels give deeper blacks and faster response. Check that the monitor supports adaptive sync (FreeSync or G-Sync) that matches your graphics card.
